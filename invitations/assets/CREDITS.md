# Invitation assets

`e36-logo.png` and `darts-beer.jpg` reuse the approved E36 United invitation assets. The old source files remain in the private local invitation evidence.

`bmw-e36-coupe-blue.webp`: photorealistic side-profile asset made with the built-in imagegen tool for this project, then encoded as WebP with its original aspect ratio and alpha. It is not described as a documentary photograph of an attendee's car. The reference was a real blue E36 M3 Coupé, photographed by Vauxford: [1998 BMW M3 Coupe 3.2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1998_BMW_M3_Coupe_3.2.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The new side profile is a generated transformation, not the original three-quarter camera view. Reference credit and license are retained here; the transformed image is provided under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.

Generation prompt: “Create ONE clean photorealistic product cutout, a TRUE ORTHOGRAPHIC SIDE PROFILE of this exact blue BMW M3 E36 two-door coupe (1990s E36/2), facing RIGHT. The reference photo is an identity and body-proportions reference, NOT a request for its three-quarter camera angle. Output a full flat side elevation, no front-facing grille or bonnet perspective, both wheels same visible size, wheels straight. Precisely preserve actual E36 coupe proportions: low angular 1990s body, long straight bonnet, short front overhang, characteristic E36 rear pillar/Hofmeister kink and cabin, long coupe door, slim black side mouldings, understated E36 bumpers, normal stock silver 17-inch wheels, correct wheelbase. Avus/Estoril BLUE body. No exaggerated rims, no lowrider, no E46/E90/modern BMW, no cartoons, no generic smooth blob, no flattened/stretched body. Realistic crisp photographic detail, evenly lit, black rubber wheels grounded at same horizontal baseline. Whole car visible with just a small transparent margin, no scene, no road, no shadow plate, no text, no badges floating outside. Genuinely transparent alpha background. Wide side-profile composition about 3.4:1 car body bounds, carefully distinguish recognisable E36 silhouette at 90px display width. This is the replacement moving car thumb for a range input, not a webpage mockup.”

`road-e36.png` is the static email illustration captured from the native HTML/CSS road with this new car. It does not make the email interactive. The webpage uses the separate moving car and native range input.
